Key Sourcing Discussion Points

A clear sourcing discussion helps connect product structure with sales channel, commercial position, and packaging needs.

  • Size platform: US, UK, EU, or custom dimensions
  • Height options: 14 inch, 18 inch, or custom height discussion
  • Structure: platform, headboard, footboard, bunk, reinforced structure
  • Surface finish: powder coating and common color direction
  • Packaging: KD / flat-pack packaging, carton planning, labels, instruction manual
  • Commercial target: commercial positioning, sales channel, quantity, and product positioning

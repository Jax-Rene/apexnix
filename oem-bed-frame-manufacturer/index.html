From Approved Direction to Production Readiness

The OEM manufacturer page focuses on the transition from an agreed product direction to repeatable manufacturing. Detailed concept development is covered in the custom bed frame development process.

  1. Confirm the Product Specification

    Align dimensions, structure, finish, support configuration, hardware and other product details.

  2. Confirm Packaging and Label Requirements

    Review carton direction, protection, hardware organization, instructions, labels and private-label artwork requirements.

  3. Prepare the Manufacturing Standard

    Use the approved product and packaging direction as the basis for production preparation.

  4. Run Production and Routine Checks

    Carry out production with routine checks covering the relevant materials, dimensions, connections, surface, assembly, hardware and packaging.

  5. Coordinate Final Review and Shipment

    Review the completed product and packaging before shipment coordination according to the agreed order requirements.

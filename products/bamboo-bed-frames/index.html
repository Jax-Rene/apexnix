Bamboo Platform Beds

A clean and natural platform bed direction for partners looking for a differentiated bed frame line.

Bamboo platform beds bring a warmer and more natural visual language compared with standard metal frames. With a clean platform structure, natural material appearance, and storage-friendly clearance, this direction is suitable for partners looking to add a softer, nature-inspired product option to their bed frame collection.

  • Natural bamboo appearance
  • Clean platform structure
  • No box spring needed

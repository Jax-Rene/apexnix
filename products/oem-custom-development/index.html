How Custom Development Works

  1. Share Your Requirement

    Tell us your product idea, sales channel, target market, quantity estimate, and customization needs.

  2. Clarify Product Direction

    We discuss suitable bed frame type, structure, material direction, packaging logic, and commercial positioning.

  3. Review Existing References

    We check whether an existing platform can be adapted, or whether a new development direction is needed.

  4. Confirm Sample Direction

    We align on structure, size, appearance, packaging, label, and key requirements before sampling.

  5. Sample & Adjustment

    Samples can be reviewed and adjusted based on assembly, structure, packaging, and market feedback.

  6. Move Toward Production

    After confirmation, we support production coordination, packaging preparation, quality control, and export communication.

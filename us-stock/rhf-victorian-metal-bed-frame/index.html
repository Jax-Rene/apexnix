Key Product Features

A clear view of the product structure, handling details and market-facing design direction.

Classical Style

Rounded rails and decorative headboard and footboard lines create a classic metal-bed look suitable for traditional and transitional bedroom collections.

Platform Support

The integrated platform structure supports practical mattress placement without requiring a separate foundation in typical platform-bed applications.

Useful Under-Bed Clearance

The raised structure leaves usable space below the frame, supporting storage-oriented bedrooms, guest rooms and rental environments.

Floor-Contact Protection

Rounded floor-contact pads help reduce direct metal contact with the floor and support more practical placement in finished interiors.

Flat-Pack Supply

The knock-down structure supports compact packing and makes the series suitable for retail, replenishment and domestic distribution.

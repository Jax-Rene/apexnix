Frequently Asked Questions

What makes ZYH different from ZYW?

ZYH emphasizes a spindle-style headboard and a more decorative furniture character, while ZYW uses a simpler wave-shaped headboard direction.

Is ZYH available from U.S. stock?

Selected ZYH sizes are included in the U.S. local stock program. Current inventory is confirmed before quotation.

Can bamboo products be customized?

U.S. stock follows existing specifications. Alternative sizes, finish details, packaging or structural changes can be discussed through a factory-direct development program.

How is domestic delivery handled?

Freight is quoted separately based on quantity and delivery ZIP code.

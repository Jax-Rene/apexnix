Frequently Asked Questions

What bed frame features matter most for online retail?

Online retail bed frames usually need compact cartons, clear instructions, easy assembly, stable structure, and photo-friendly product stories.

Can Apexnix support private label packaging for e-commerce sellers?

Yes. Packaging discussion can include carton marks, barcode labels, instruction manuals, protection details, and private label presentation.

Which product direction is best for a first online bed frame line?

Most new online lines start with practical metal platform bed frames, then add bamboo or headboard options once positioning is clear.
